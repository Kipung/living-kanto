# Source residents, counter posts and battle venues

The October 6 fidelity update restores unconditional mainland FireRed character objects at their source positions and facing using the pinned imported map data and original sprite descriptors. This supplements the existing 100 local-model autonomous humans with persistent source NPCs, rather than inventing model decisions, parties, histories or achievements for the original game cast. Source NPCs can be selected in the existing person panel, searched in People & NPCs and read in the same saved history. Safe direct source dialogue is available as an adjacent interaction and retained in the listener's memory and canonical event history. Conditional scripts are not executed or guessed. The manifest retains map/script/text paths and SHA provenance. Source flag-controlled campaign appearances are omitted; unconditional source residents remain stationary in this initial version. Source wandering, full original trainer-team battles, quest scripts and per-trainer original dialogue branches remain fidelity gaps.

Existing service assignments replace original nurses/clerks, and officeholder/fossil/Dojo equivalents are suppressed rather than duplicated. Residents have authoritative collision and source elevations. An existing human on a source resident's origin can step away; the overlapping source sprite is suppressed until then. Adding residents never relocates an active human to make room.

Nurses have distinct counter posts on the actual source nurse floor and its adjacent staff tile, facing south. The general population placement no longer pushes them into the lobby just because the enclosed post lacks four open neighbours. On-duty nurses stay at the post and can rest, socialize or choose service there. Healing service and relief coverage require the assigned post. Another available nurse permits a break. `take_service_post` and `leave_service_post` are explicit human choices with recorded five-second movement and occupied-destination validation. This staff-only passage across the source counter is a documented shared-world adaptation; it does not make the counter walkable for customers. Busy people keep existing activities and later choose their return. Idle same-building nurses are moved to the post by the identified user intervention.

New personal duels require a source-authorized venue: outdoors, caves, gyms, Dojo, League chambers and source indoor trainer venues. Houses, labs, shops and Pokémon Center lobbies reject casual proposals and acceptances. Venue is checked again at acceptance, including in the battle factory. Official/source special battle authorization is separate. Battles already recorded in now-peaceful interiors continue to completion; migration does not manufacture an outcome or discard their history.

The update is one audited intervention preserving every existing human ID, Pokémon, inventory, memories, relationships, ongoing activities, battle state and old event. It does not reset the world or grant progression. Source residents and their provenance are stored in `WorldState.npcs`, and migration replay is verified through the canonical store.

Route audit found Route13's source Perry placement is a narrow-lane obstruction. A nearby person can explicitly ask a source resident to make way. This is a recorded shared-world courtesy adaptation rather than an invented battle or unrecorded relocation. The engine chooses a lawful free perpendicular recess; if there is none, both participants may exchange adjacent tiles only when both source steps are legal. Staff posts, other people, environment objects, water and exits are protected. Source NPC movement otherwise remains stationary. This lets the route remain usable while retaining normal character collision. Inference uses current resident/occupancy validation before committing the passage.
