# Purpose and concrete next steps

People retain their existing aspirations, commitments, identities and history. `individual_life.task` adds one optional model-selected concrete next step beneath a commitment. It records the chosen executable action and arguments, purpose, expected factual result, baseline, lifecycle and latest decision explanation. `plan_next_step` is planning only; the person must subsequently choose the actual action. Planning candidates come from the final bounded legal menu, with six diverse action/destination kinds. No automatic human actions or rewards are introduced.

Supported factual step outcomes are arrival at a specific map or coordinate, party acquisition, service completion, completed work/rest activity, and resolved training battle. Deferred movement, queues and activities remain in progress until their exact engine result is observed. Service/activity receipts use the accepted operation ID; unrelated earlier receipts cannot complete a new task. Battle resolution does not imply victory. The engine records step completion in the same canonical transaction as its result. Free-text commitment completion remains a separate self-assessment.

People may pause, resume or abandon a task with a recorded reason. Other action choices retain the task and record their explanation, labelled as a different choice rather than asserting semantic relevance. A cancelled/ended operation without its expected result records a blocked execution state. Workplace restrictions and currently unavailable planned actions appear in the owner's continuity observation. A blocked task does not relax duty, collision, care, or ownership rules. Existing pending activities continue unchanged through source updates.

`respond_to` links an utterance to a privately delivered speech ID, with an explicit answer, acknowledgement or decline disposition. Both participants retain the receipt. This records which message a person intended to address, not whether their answer is correct or relevant; `semantic_answer_verified` remains false. Normal speech and disengagement remain available. No retroactive speech delivery or reply reconstruction is performed.

Repeated `remember` or `set_goal` choices now produce factual-progress guidance even when text varies. Role-aware provider guidance invites people to reconsider inherited aspirations themselves; no biographies, family facts, goals or achievements are rewritten. Feedback and model-selected plans do not guarantee loop escape.

Task history and recent choices are bounded in private observations; full canonical history remains intact. Provenance is removed from private autobiographical observations. Planning/reply contracts are additive; old saves and recorded event patches require no destructive migration. Planning and reply options fit within the 128-option cap. Route-dependent planning checks use the complete menu and stale dependency refresh. Observer profiles show purpose, next step, actual status, blocker, latest reason and previous steps within the existing person view.
