No row establishes the full release gate. Initial roles, declared companions, Creative fixtures and scripted test minds are excluded from autonomous achievement evidence.

Explicit mainland adaptations: Snorlax clearance is per trainer, essential source pickups/stations are repeatable per trainer, the initial champion is declared setup, personal duels use linked-battle reward rules, and Mewtwo requires the trainer’s Hall of Fame entry in place of the excluded Sevii network quest. These do not award badges or experience automatically. Source Moltres is on excluded Sevii Mt. Ember; no mainland substitute is fabricated.

The Lift Key source item ball is made available once per trainer at its original Hideout B4F coordinates because the Rocket campaign is excluded. The engine does not invent a grunt victory. FireRed encounter slot probabilities remain the baseline; distinct LeafGreen variants of the same slot share availability.

Rod gifts, Everstone, Itemfinder, Amulet Coin, Exp. Share and Psychic TM29 use per-trainer environmental stations at the original source interaction coordinates. Source distinct caught-species requirements (20/30/40/50 respectively) remain enforced. Thirty-second gift interaction time is a simulation adaptation. Station tests check source coordinates, rewards, thresholds, physical proximity and independent one-time collection; no NPC dialogue or achievement is fabricated.

Detailed battle boundaries and source hook coverage are recorded in [MECHANICS_FIDELITY.md](MECHANICS_FIDELITY.md). Engine replay is canonical deterministic replay, not bit-identical emulation of the cartridge global random stream.
